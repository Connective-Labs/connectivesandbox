# What Connective Sandbox can do

A plain-language guide for reps and prospects. Paste it verbatim into an email
or a walkthrough. Last updated: Phase 3 (template library + planner).

## Plan it for me

Describe the client in a few lines — GLM plans the build: which template to
start from, which modules it needs, the wording to customise for this client,
and what is still open. You review the plan, test the draft, and publish.
Nothing ships itself.

## Start from a recipe or the library

You never start from a blank page:

- **Plan it for me** — GLM plans the whole build from a short brief (and any
  recent recorded discovery call).
- **Named recipes** — Photo triage, Document intake review, Operations desk,
  Approval desk.
- **Your library** — every workflow can be saved as a reusable template.
  Pick one, tweak the client-specific copy (names, price bands, capture
  hints), and it becomes the new client's workflow. Improved builds save back
  as new template versions, so the library gets better with every client.

## The modules

Every workflow is assembled from a fixed set of approved modules. They fall
into two groups:

**Collect** — how your customers hand over information:

- **Photos** — customers upload photos straight from their phone.
- **Forms** — structured fields for the details you always need.
- **Chat** — a message thread, like WhatsApp, with attachments.
- **Choices** — taps, not typing — clear options for common answers.

**Show** — what your team sees once the AI has judged a submission:

- **Verdicts** — one big verdict chip: quotable, needs a visit, and so on.
- **Quotes** — a draft quote with price bands, ready for your check.
- **Queues** — every job on one sortable list with two clear buttons.
- **Alerts** — what is overdue or going wrong, flagged in one feed.
- **Pipelines** — where each order stands, from received to done.
- **Meters** — how confident the AI was, next to every decision.

Every workflow also carries the human-ownership trail (a decision log) and a
usage counter, so you can always see who decided what, and when.

## Good to know

- **Workflows are assembled from approved modules.** A brand-new module goes
  through the engineering backlog first — the builder cannot invent one.
- **Client data stays scoped to the client.** One client never sees another's
  anything.
- **Every AI change passes through the rep before it reaches the client.**
  Nothing ships itself — you are the gate.
- **Live transcription builds as you talk.** Record the discovery call and the
  draft takes shape while you speak; wording streams in as the AI writes it.

## Current limits

- **No new custom modules from chat.** The builder can arrange, word, and tune
  the approved modules — it cannot create a module type that does not exist
  yet. New module types are an engineering backlog decision.
- **Integrations are representation-only until built.** For example, a queue
  row can *show* that a job came from WhatsApp or Shopify, but Connective
  Sandbox does not yet *send* WhatsApp messages or write back to Shopify. The
  screens demonstrate the workflow shape; the pipes come when built.
- **Judges decide within closed option sets.** The AI picks among answers you
  approved; it never free-texts a conclusion into your workflow.
- **Published changes are versioned, not silently edited.** The client sees a
  change only after you publish a reviewed draft.
